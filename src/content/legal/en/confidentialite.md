Clickers Community takes the protection of your personal data seriously. This page explains what data we collect, why, and how to exercise your rights.

## Data controller

Clickers Community, reachable at [communityclickers50@gmail.com](mailto:communityclickers50@gmail.com).

## Data collected

We only collect the data you choose to send us:

- through the **contact form**: name, email address, phone (optional), organisation (optional), subject and message;
- through the **quote request form**: organisation, type of organisation, service required, context, indicative budget, timeframe, name, email address and phone (optional);
- through the **evaluation form (client reviews)**: name, organisation and job title (optional), email address, project, ratings, recommendation and comments. Your review is only published on the website if you tick the publication consent box;
- through the **newsletter sign-up**: email address. You can unsubscribe at any time;
- when you write to us by **email** or on **WhatsApp**. WhatsApp conversations are also subject to that service's privacy policy.

## Purposes and legal basis

Your data is used only to answer your requests, prepare proposals and quotes, and arrange meetings. This processing is based on your consent, given by ticking the box in the forms, and on the pre-contractual steps you request.

Meeting bookings are handled by **Cal.com**. The calendar only loads when you click "Book a meeting". The details you enter to book (name, email, time slot) are then processed by Cal.com under its own privacy policy, and may involve cookies required for the service to work.

## Your rights

Under applicable data protection law (including the GDPR for people located in the European Union), you have the right to access, rectify, erase and object to the processing of your data, and to withdraw your consent at any time. To exercise these rights, write to [communityclickers50@gmail.com](mailto:communityclickers50@gmail.com).
