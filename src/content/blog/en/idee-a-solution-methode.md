Too often, a good idea gets stuck for lack of technical support. At Clickers Community, every project follows a clear process designed to reduce risk and deliver a useful solution.

## 1. Analyze the needs

It all starts with listening. We meet your teams, understand your context and set goals together: what problem should the solution solve, for whom, and how will we measure success?

## 2. Design the solution

We design the user experience (journeys, mockups) and the technical architecture (data, APIs, hosting). You approve before a single line of code is written.

## 3. Develop step by step

Development moves forward in short iterations. At each step we share progress and include your feedback: no bad surprises at delivery.

## 4. Test rigorously

Quality is not a bonus. Functional tests, mobile tests, data checks: the solution is proven before it goes live.

## 5. Deploy

We put the solution online, in the cloud or on your servers, and train the users.

## 6. Follow up

After delivery we stay by your side to keep performance high and bring useful improvements.
