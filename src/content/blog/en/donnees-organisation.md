Data is at the heart of digital. Yet many organizations still manage their information in scattered files. Here are five signs it is time to move to a structured database.

## 1. Your information lives in dozens of files

Each department has its own spreadsheet, with its own version. Nobody knows which one is up to date.

## 2. Duplicates and errors keep piling up

The same beneficiary appears three times with three different spellings. The numbers no longer match.

## 3. Preparing a report takes days

Compiling data for a donor or management requires long, risky manual work.

## 4. You are afraid of losing your data

No backups, uncontrolled access: one lost laptop can be enough to erase everything.

## 5. You cannot quickly answer a simple question

"How many cases did we handle this quarter?" should not take half a day.

## The solution

A well-designed database centralizes information, protects access and powers up-to-date dashboards. It is the foundation of any digital transformation.
